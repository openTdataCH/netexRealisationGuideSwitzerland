Kurswagen (Direct carriage)

Overview

A Kurswagen (direct carriage) is a coach group that is pulled by different trains on different sections of its route. A passenger stays seated in the coach group from its first to its last stop and books exactly this journey; the pulling trains are separate journeys in their own right.

Example used in this use case: direct carriage 37 Zürich HB – Bremen Hbf, taken from the direct-carriage example of the SKI HRDF realisation guide (section 7.1.4). The coach group is pulled by train 472 (Zürich HB – Basel Bad Bf), train 470 (Basel Bad Bf – Hannover Hbf) and train 2746 (Hannover Hbf – Bremen Hbf). Train 470 continues to Hamburg-Altona after the coach group has been detached.

Mapping between HRDF and NeTEx

HRDF NeTEx RG1 NeTEx RG2 Use Case
*KW / *KWZ – ServiceJourney for the coach group with one JourneyPart (PurposeOfJourneyPartition:DirectCarriage) per *KWZ Direct carriage
Attribute KW (“Direkter Wagen”) – Notice assigned to the ServiceJourney of the coach group (see mapping excel, sheet Angebote) Passenger-facing marker

In HRDF, *KW carries the number of the direct carriage. Each section follows as a *KWZ line with the train number of the pulling train and the from and to stations:

*KW  000037
*KWZ 000472 80____ Zuerich HB    - Basel Bad Bf   02215 02319
*KWZ 000470 80____ Basel Bad Bf  - Hannover Hbf   02340 00612
*KWZ 002746 80____ Hannover Hbf  - Bremen Hbf     00644 00754

Modelling with NeTEx RG 2.0 (JourneyPart)

Direct carriages are modelled with JourneyPart. The general rules for JourneyPart are described in uc05 Journey Parts.

  • The coach group is one complete ServiceJourney from its first to its last stop. It has its own ServiceJourneyPattern over the whole path, its own TimeDemandType (the wait times are the handover dwells) and its own Line, because RouteView/LineRef is mandatory on ServiceJourneyPattern.
  • Each pulling train is a ServiceJourney of its own, with its own ServiceJourneyPattern.
  • The ServiceJourney of the coach group contains one JourneyPart per pulling train, i.e. one per *KWZ:
    • ParentJourneyRef references the ServiceJourney of the pulling train.
    • FromPointInPatternRef / ToPointInPatternRef delimit the section within the ServiceJourneyPattern of the pulling train. This can be a sub-range: train 470 has three points (Basel Bad Bf, Hannover Hbf, Hamburg-Altona), the coach group only rides points 1 to 2.
    • TrainNumberRef is the train number of the pulling train.
    • StartTime / EndTime with the day offsets give the time bounds of the section.
    • PurposeOfJourneyPartitionRef is ch:1:PurposeOfJourneyPartition:DirectCarriage.
  • The coach group has no train number of its own. Its trainNumbers list the TrainNumbers of the pulling trains in the order of the sections. The number of the direct carriage from *KW is not a TrainNumber: a TrainNumber is the number from *Z (see TrainNumber template).
  • JourneyPartCouple and ServiceJourneyInterchange are not used for direct carriages. ParentJourneyRef together with the two PointInPattern references already states which train pulls which section.
